Data rules

The short version

Claude at JHU may be used with FERPA-protected student records. It is not HIPAA-compliant: never put patient data or protected health information (PHI) into it. IT@JH's Claude page lists which data may go in and is the authority.

See the IT@JH Claude page and the IT@JH guidelines for responsible use of AI. Background: what FERPA is (University Registrar) and health information privacy (Johns Hopkins Medicine).

What may go in

Kind of dataMay it go into Claude?Notes
Published sources and your own draftsYes.—
Student records (FERPA)Yes, in JHU's Claude only.Not personal accounts. Share only what the task needs.
Patient data and protected health information (PHI)No.Claude is not HIPAA-compliant.
IRB human-subjects dataOnly as your approved protocol allows.Ask your IRB before using AI with human-subjects data.
Controlled-access data (for example genomic data under a data use agreement), export-controlled data, restricted archival collectionsNo, unless the agreement says so.Read the agreement.
Personnel files and confidential lettersNo.—

Use a JHU account, not a personal one

Only JHU's Claude carries the university's data protections.

When in doubt, leave it out and ask

Ask the Genius Bar or IT@JH.
