Licenses and access

Which tools are approved, how to get a Claude license, and who pays.

Approved tools

JHU's approved AI tools include Claude and HopGPT. See the IT@JH AI page (JHU).

Claude licenses

Claude licenses come through IT@JH. The IT@JH Claude page (JHU) lists license types, costs, allowed data and how to request one.

The request form is the enterprise AI license form On campus or JH VPN only.

The Mid and Advanced tiers need a license that includes Claude Code. The IT@JH page lists which ones do.

How paying works

A license works like parking. It is a university service each person can choose to buy. Unlike parking, it can be charged to a cost center.

FacultyWho paysHow
Faculty with research budgets (grants, start-up, research or discretionary accounts)Their own cost center, where the sponsor's rules allow.Research administration confirms which grants do.
Faculty without research budgetsTheir department or school, if it chooses.Ask your chair.
Anyone who chooses not to buyNo one.Demos, recordings and self-service need no license. Small groups and the Mid tier do.
