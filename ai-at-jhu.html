AI at JHU

A directory of AI pages across the university. Each link goes to the office that owns it.

Access and rules

  • Artificial Intelligence (GenAI@JH) (IT@JH). JHU IT artificial intelligence hub with an overview of generative AI at Johns Hopkins.
  • Claude Licenses for JHU (IT@JH). JHU IT page on Claude licenses for faculty, staff and students.
  • Guidelines for Responsible Use of AI (IT@JH). JHU IT guidelines for responsible use of AI across Johns Hopkins University and Medicine.
  • Generative Artificial Intelligence at Hopkins (GenAI at Hopkins). JHU generative AI hub with community resources and a link to launch HopGPT.
  • Generative AI and LLMs: ChatGPT, Claude, HopGPT (Whiting IT). Whiting IT guide to using generative AI and LLMs such as ChatGPT, Claude and HopGPT.
  • HopGPT (IT@JH). HopGPT login page for JHU access to language models from OpenAI, Anthropic and Meta.
  • Enterprise AI license form (JHU) On campus or JH VPN only. Request form for an enterprise AI license.

Research computing

  • Artificial Intelligence (Research IT). Research IT page on AI support for research at Johns Hopkins.
  • HopGPT API access (IT@JH). HopGPT support page on API access to JHU's language models.
  • ARCH Clusters Guide (ARCH). Documentation for JHU's ARCH research computing clusters.
  • Data Science and AI Institute (DSAI) (DSAI). Home of the Data Science and AI Institute, with research, education and news.

Libraries

  • AI Foundations for Academic Work guide (Sheridan Libraries). Library guide on what AI is, how it works and how to evaluate its outputs.
  • AI Tools for Research and Scholarship guide (Sheridan Libraries). Library guide to AI tools for research and scholarship.
  • Artificial Intelligence in Academic Work (Sheridan Libraries). Sheridan Libraries page listing AI support services for academic work.
  • Data Services (Sheridan Libraries). Data Services home for data management, GIS and research data support.
  • GitHub Copilot for JHU (Open Source Programs Office). Open Source Programs Office page on getting GitHub Copilot for JHU.

Teaching

  • Center for Teaching Excellence and Innovation (CTEI) (CTEI). CTEI home with teaching programs, tools, a teaching blog and events.
  • Teaching and AI (CTEI). CTEI guidance on AI in teaching: approved tools, prompting tips, disclosure and AI detectors.
  • Teaching Fellows: page Coming soon

Privacy and compliance

  • What is FERPA (University Registrar). Registrar page explaining what FERPA is and how it applies at Johns Hopkins.
  • Patient Privacy Information (Johns Hopkins Medicine). Johns Hopkins Medicine public page on health information privacy and its privacy notice.
  • IRB and the Data Trust: ask your IRB about AI in human-subjects research.
